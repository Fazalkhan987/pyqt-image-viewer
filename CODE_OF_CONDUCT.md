# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Lumina project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on the issue rather than the person.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Protect private or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or project work.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Reporting problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on the repository. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible. The maintainer may investigate, ask for additional information, and take action that can include removing content, closing discussions, or restricting participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to behavior that affects the safety or inclusion of the Lumina community, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This Code of Conduct is intended to support a respectful community and may be updated as the project grows.
